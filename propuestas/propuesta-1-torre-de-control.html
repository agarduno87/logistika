The problem

Nobody holds the whole operation in their head.

The customs broker sees their piece. The forwarder sees theirs. The carrier, the supplier and the warehouse see theirs. When nobody sees the whole, the cost only shows up at the end — and it shows up on your P&L.

COST

Charges nobody forecast: demurrage, storage, handling, reclassifications. Discovered once they have already been paid.

TIME

Transits that stretch because a document arrived late and nobody was watching for it.

COMPLIANCE

Incomplete files, questionable tariff codes and permits reviewed only when the authority asks.

DEPENDENCY

The whole operation lives in one person. If they leave or fall ill, it stops.
