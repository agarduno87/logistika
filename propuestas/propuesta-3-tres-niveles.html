Questions

What we get asked on every first call.

Are you a customs broker?

No, and the distinction matters. In Mexico, customs clearance is performed by a licensed customs broker. logistika coordinates, supervises and audits that broker, and answers for the result of the whole operation — but does not replace their legal function or charge for it.

Are you a carrier or a forwarder?

Also no. We own no trucks, ships or warehouses, and that is an advantage: we can select and compare vendors based on your operation rather than on what we have sitting idle in a yard.

What size of company do you serve?

The model works best for industrial companies of 20 to 300 employees, with international operations and no specialized foreign trade function — or with one saturated person doing all of it.

How do you charge?

It depends on the level: START is a fixed-price project, MANAGE is a monthly retainer plus a fee per shipment, and PARTNER is a monthly retainer on an annual agreement. In all three the scope and the price are agreed in writing before work begins.

What happens to our current customs broker?

They stay, if they perform. Part of the work is evaluating them with data — times, charges, incidents — and telling you honestly whether to keep, renegotiate or compare. Changing vendors is not a goal, it is a possible conclusion.

Do you work with foreign companies entering Mexico?

Yes, and it is one of the most natural fits: a company from Europe, Asia or the United States that will sell or manufacture here needs someone to run the Mexican side of the chain from the first shipment.
